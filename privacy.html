Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026
App: MigraSky: Migraine Diary for iPhone
Published by: Ali Alhosani
Contact: achetrack@outlook.com

Summary

MigraSky keeps what you enter on your iPhone. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive your diary. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share a file with the iOS share sheet.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is stored on your iPhone, in the app's private storage and in the app's settings:

  • Diary entries: start and end time, pain level, places on the head, symptoms, possible triggers, things you tried, medication text, your own note on how medication went, free-text notes, and (if you allow them) an air-pressure value and approximate hours of sleep.
  • Medication: the medications you add (name, detail, optional refill date) and the doses you log.
  • Doctor visits: the notes you write (date, doctor or clinic name, summary, medicine changes, things to remember, next appointment) and, if you choose, a small count of your diary saved with the visit.
  • Quiet days and breathing days: the days you marked headache-free and the days you used the breathing guide.
  • Settings and choices: language, theme, whether the Face ID lock, reminders, tips and the Lock Screen timer are on, your report name and questions for your doctor, your first-launch answers (usual medication and triggers), an optional first name (only used to say hello on the Today screen), and the look of your drawn character.
  • Widget summary: a very small summary (whether an episode is running and since when, how many days you logged, your last seven days as colors) saved in an App Group so the app's own widgets can show it. It can be read only by this app and its widgets. When the Face ID lock is on it contains no personal details.

Does anything leave your device?

No. The app contains no networking code, no web views and no links that open websites. It does not send your data to the developer, to a server or to any third party, and it uses no third-party code libraries.

You can choose to share something yourself: a PDF report, a CSV file, a month picture or a doctor-visit note, using the iOS share sheet. Only the person or app you pick receives it.

Permissions

All are optional, and the app works if you decline them.

  • Face ID / Touch ID: only if you turn on "Lock with Face ID". iOS does the check and tells the app only yes or no. The app never receives biometric data. Your device passcode is the backup.
  • Motion & Fitness (air pressure): when an episode starts and "Note air pressure" is on (it is on by default), the app reads the iPhone barometer once and saves the value as a note on that entry. Turn it off in Settings → "Note air pressure".
  • Apple Health (sleep, read-only): off by default. If you turn on "Note sleep from Apple Health", iOS asks you for permission to let the app read sleep analysis. The app reads the previous 24 hours when you start an episode and saves approximate hours slept as a note. The app never writes to Health, does not read anything else from Health, and does not use Health data for advertising or share it with anyone. You can withdraw access in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.
  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on a reminder (daily reminder, medication refill, doctor appointment). They are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, with no server involved.
  • Live Activity (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island timer): an optional timer for a running episode. It is updated only by the app on your iPhone, with no push service. With the Face ID lock on, it shows no pain level.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and the Action button: you can start or end an episode, or set the pain level, by voice or shortcut. iOS and Siri handle the speech under Apple's own terms; the app only receives the request and changes your diary on the device.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app has no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting tools of its own, does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you have chosen to share iPhone analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash or usage statistics to the developer; the app itself does not collect or send them.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup for your iPhone, it may include this app's data. That is controlled by your device settings and Apple's services, not by the app.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → "Import & export (CSV)" saves your entries as a CSV file.
  • Erase: Settings → "Erase all entries" removes every entry, dose, medication and doctor visit stored by the app. Deleting the app removes the rest of its data from your iPhone.
  • Permissions: change or withdraw any permission at any time in iPhone Settings.

Because the developer never receives your information, there is no developer-held copy to access, correct or delete. Your data is in your hands on your device.

Children

The app is a general health diary and is not designed for children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

MigraSky is a personal diary and tracking tool. It does not diagnose, give medical advice or recommend treatment, and it does not replace a healthcare professional. Charts and reports only summarize what you entered. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same web address with a new date, and the in-app Privacy policy (Settings → About) will be updated with the app.
