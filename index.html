MigraSky: Migraine Diary

A simple, private migraine tracker and headache diary for iPhone. Log an episode in one tap, add details when you feel up to it, and keep everything on your device.

What it does

  • One big button starts a timer for a new episode; one tap ends it.
  • Log pain level, where it hurts, symptoms, possible triggers, things you tried and medication.
  • See your own entries in a history list, a calendar and simple charts.
  • Keep medication doses and doctor-visit notes, and create a PDF report to share.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts.

Private by design

No account, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Your diary is stored on your iPhone only. Read the full privacy policy.

Not medical advice. MigraSky is a personal diary. It does not diagnose, give medical advice or recommend treatment. Please talk to a healthcare professional about your health.
