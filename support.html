Support

MigraSky: Migraine Diary is a simple, private diary for logging migraines and headaches on your iPhone. Your entries stay on your device.

Not medical advice. The app only records and summarizes what you enter. It does not diagnose, give medical advice or recommend treatment. Please talk to a healthcare professional about your health. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Common questions

How do I start and end an episode?

On the Today tab, tap the big round button to start the timer. Tap "It has ended" when it is over. You can add details right away or later from the History tab.

How do I add a past episode?

Open the History tab and tap the + button. Choose the start and end time and fill in what you remember.

My entries are missing
  • Check the History tab and the Calendar. The calendar shows one month at a time; use the arrows to move between months.
  • If you deleted the app, its data was removed from the iPhone. Restoring from an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) can bring it back if the backup was made before you deleted it.
  • If you used Settings → "Erase all entries", the entries cannot be restored.
How do I make a report for my doctor?

Settings → "Create a PDF report". Choose the dates, review the preview and use Share to send it. The report is made on your iPhone and is only shared if you choose to share it.

How do I back up or move my diary?

Settings → "Import & export (CSV)" saves your entries as a file you can keep or import again. An iPhone backup also includes the app's data.

How do I change the language or theme?

Settings → Language and Appearance. A language change may need the app to be closed and opened again.

How do I change my name or my character?

Settings → "Your character" changes how your character looks. Settings → "Your first name" changes the name used to say hello on the Today screen. Both are optional and stay on your iPhone.

Permissions troubleshooting

Every permission is optional and can be changed in the iPhone Settings app → scroll down and tap the app's name, or in the places listed below.

Face ID lock does not work or cannot be turned on
  • Face ID or Touch ID and a passcode must be set up on the iPhone: Settings → Face ID & Passcode.
  • Check the app has Face ID access: iPhone Settings → the app's name → Face ID.
  • If Face ID does not recognize you, the app accepts your device passcode instead.
  • The lock is turned on and off in the app under Settings → "Lock with Face ID".
Reminders do not appear
  • Turn the reminder on in the app: Settings → "Daily reminder" (for medication refills and doctor appointments, in those screens).
  • Allow notifications: iPhone Settings → Notifications → the app's name → Allow Notifications.
  • Focus modes, Do Not Disturb and Notification Summary can hide or delay notifications. Check Settings → Focus.
Sleep from Apple Health is not noted
  • Turn on Settings → "Note sleep from Apple Health" in the app and allow the Sleep permission when iOS asks.
  • To change it later: Health app → your profile picture → Privacy → Apps → the app's name → Sleep.
  • The app only reads sleep data from the previous 24 hours when you start an episode. If your iPhone or Apple Watch recorded no sleep, nothing is noted. It never writes to Health.
Air pressure is not noted
  • Make sure "Note air pressure" is on in the app under Settings.
  • Allow Motion & Fitness: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → the app's name.
  • The barometer is read once when an episode starts. If it cannot be read within a few seconds, the note is left out.
Widgets or the Lock Screen timer do not show
  • Add a widget: touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen → Edit → add this app's widget from the list.
  • For the Lock Screen timer, turn on Settings → "Show the timer on the Lock Screen" in the app, and make sure Live Activities are allowed in iPhone Settings → the app's name.
  • With the Face ID lock on, widgets and the timer deliberately show no personal details.
Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button
  • Say "Start a migraine in" followed by the app's name, or add the actions in the Shortcuts app.
  • On iPhones with an Action button: iPhone Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → choose the app's start or end action.
  • If Siri says the diary could not be opened, open the app once and try again.

Report a bug or send feedback

Please email achetrack@outlook.com and include:

  1. What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  2. Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  3. The app version (in the app: Settings → About → Version).
  4. A screenshot if it helps. Please do not send your diary entries or other health details; describe the problem without them.

We cannot see your diary, because it never leaves your iPhone. That is why the steps above matter.
